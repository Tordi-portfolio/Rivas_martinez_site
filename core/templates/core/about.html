Our story

{{ biz.name }} started with a straightforward idea: people deserve a cleaning service they never have to worry about. Too many companies send a different stranger each week, rush through the job and disappear when something goes wrong. We chose to do it differently.

We build small, consistent teams so you see familiar faces. We train every cleaner in the same room-by-room method so quality does not depend on who shows up. And we keep our door open at all hours, because life does not only happen between nine and five.

Based on Arvana Street in Houston, we serve families, renters, landlords and local businesses who want the same thing: a space that is clean, calm and ready to use.
